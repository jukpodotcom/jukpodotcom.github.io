How it works

From curious to confident, one idea at a time.

  1. ScreenshotNew space: a topic typed in, areas being drafted

    Start a Space

    Name any topic. Jukpo drafts its areas, the ideas in each and their key points, plus a big picture of how it fits together.

  2. ScreenshotA lesson card, with a highlight and a note beside it

    Read a short lesson

    Each idea gets a lesson in cards, written to scan and then read. Highlight, take notes, or ask the tutor to explain something more simply.

  3. ScreenshotA practice question, answered, with feedback

    Practice until it sticks

    Answer questions written from the lesson. A daily sitting brings back what’s due, so what you learn comes back when you need it.

Features

Built for learning, not just reading.

A map of every topic

See a Space as a graph of its areas and ideas, and how each idea stands.

Everything due today

Sittings choose what to look at today: new ideas to start and old ones to recall.

Many kinds of question

Tap, sort, match, fill a gap, predict, explain it back, spot the flaw, write code.

Notes in Markdown

Highlights and notes sit beside the lesson and are kept as plain Markdown files.

A tutor on hand

Select any words in a lesson to ask about them or have them explained more simply.

Grows with you

When an idea or area gets too big, Jukpo offers to split it. Nothing changes until you say yes.

Bring your own model

Uses the AI you already have.

Jukpo writes lessons, questions and feedback with a model you connect. Sign in with a tool you already use, or point it at any OpenAI-compatible server, including one on your own computer.

Your record, your files

Everything you learn stays in a folder you choose.

  • Plain files on your computer. Your progress is a log that is only ever added to; notes are Markdown.
  • Sync it your way. Put the folder in Syncthing or another sync tool to learn on more than one computer.
  • Nothing is lost by accident. Spaces are archived, not deleted, until you ask to delete one forever.

Open source

MIT-licensed. Take it, change it, make it your own.

Jukpo is Electron and React over a plain TypeScript core. Contributions are welcome; the contributing guide explains the layout and the rules.
