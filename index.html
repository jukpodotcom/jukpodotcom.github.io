How it works

From curious to confident, one idea at a time.

  1. 01

    Say what you want to learn

    A subject, a book or course, or something you want to be able to do. Jukpo names the Space and sketches its areas, then drafts the ideas in each one.

  2. 02

    Read a short lesson

    Every idea gets a lesson in cards, written to be scanned and then read. Highlight a line, write a note beside it, or ask the tutor to explain it more simply.

  3. 03

    Practice until it sticks

    Questions are written from the lesson you read, and a wrong answer comes with the reason. Start a sitting and Jukpo picks what to look at today.

Features

Built for learning, not just reading.

A map of everything you’re learning

Each Space is a graph of its areas and ideas. Home draws them all, with the bridges between subjects.

Notes in Markdown

A notebook for each Space and a note for each idea, with checklists, maths and code.

Many kinds of question

Choose, sort, match, order, fill a gap, predict, explain it back, spot the flaw, write code.

A tutor on hand

Select any words in a lesson to ask about them, or have them explained more simply.

One button for today

Start a sitting and Jukpo chooses: new ideas to begin, and old ones that are due to come back.

Grows with you

When an idea gets too big, Jukpo offers to split it. Nothing changes until you say yes.

Bring your own model

Uses the AI you already have.

  • Sign in with a tool you use. Claude Code, Codex or Antigravity, through the command-line tool already on your computer.
  • Or point it at a server. Any OpenAI-compatible one, hosted or running on your own machine.
  • Choose which model does what. A small one for quick reads, your most capable for writing lessons.

Your record, your files

What you learn stays in a folder you choose.

  • Plain files, no Jukpo account. Progress is a log that is only ever added to; lessons and notes are Markdown.
  • Sync it your way. Put the folder in Syncthing or another sync tool to learn on more than one computer.
  • Nothing is lost by accident. A Space is archived, not deleted, until you ask to delete it forever.
record/spaces/<space>/
├─ log/<device>/2026-11.jsonl   # every change
├─ lessons/<idea>/<hash>.md     # each lesson
└─ notes/
   ├─ space.md                  # the notebook
   └─ ideas/<idea>.md           # a note per idea
