Jukpo — wordmark

Round 3: wide and tight, optically spaced. “ukpo” drawn by hand from the app icon’s j — no font. Same 14 stroke, flat cut ends, round bowls; the j’s flat top is the x-height and its hook sits on the baseline. Each option is shown on paper, on ink and on the Sunrise gradient, then as a site header, beside the Sunrise app icon, and at 32, 20 and 14 px.
