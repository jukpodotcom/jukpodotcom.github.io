Jukpo — serif wordmark

A serif study of wordmark #4 (wider k, shorter stem). Card 1 is #4 itself, the sans reference; the others put serifs on its exact skeleton: same 14 stroke, x-height, baseline, wide rounds and k. Serifs sit where a serif face puts them: on the tops of u’s stems, k’s stem top and foot, the ends of k’s arm and leg, p’s stem top and foot; o has none.

Serifs change the letters’ sides, so every option is re-spaced by the same area method as #4 (gap 9, depth 4), measured on the serif shapes. Each option on paper and ink, as a site header, beside the black and white icons, and at 32, 20 and 14 px.
